# Teclury – Company Knowledge Base (RAG Document)

This document contains structured knowledge about Teclury, its services, technology stack, processes, pricing approach, AI capabilities, and support model.

## I. Company Overview (The “Who”)

### Tell me about your company

Teclury is a technology-driven software and AI solutions company focused on building **production-ready digital products** for startups, small businesses, and growing teams.
We work as a **development partner**, not just a delivery vendor, helping clients move from idea to MVP, from MVP to pilot, and from pilot to scalable product.

Our philosophy is simple: **we build software that works** — reliable, maintainable, and ready for real-world use.

---

### Where are you located?

Teclury operates as a **remote-first, distributed engineering team** with its core engineering base in **India**.
This allows us to combine strong technical talent with cost efficiency while serving clients globally through digital collaboration tools like email, WhatsApp, and video calls.

---

### How long have you been in business?

Teclury was founded as a student-led engineering initiative and has evolved into a professional software and AI development company.
Our experience comes not only from client projects but also from building and operating our **own AI products and platforms**.

---

### Are you a startup or an established enterprise?

Teclury is a **growing technology company** — not a large enterprise consultancy, but more than a freelance setup.
This gives clients the advantage of:

* Direct access to engineers
* Faster execution
* Founder-level involvement
* Flexible engagement models

---

### Do you have any certifications or awards?

Rather than focusing on industry awards, Teclury prioritizes **client success and production outcomes**.
Our credibility comes from:

* Deploying real, working software
* Building and maintaining our own AI products
* Open-source contributions through our AI Lab
* Long-term client partnerships

Our belief is that **client growth is the real award**.

---

### Why should I choose you over other IT agencies?

Clients choose Teclury because:

* We focus on **execution, not just presentations**
* We combine **full-stack engineering + AI expertise**
* We are transparent, fast, and technically hands-on
* We think long-term, not just project delivery
* We are cost-effective without cutting corners

We aim to act as a **technical partner or fractional CTO**, not a task-based vendor.
